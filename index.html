Why we are building this

From overwhelming data to useful decisions.

Racing setup adjustments are complex. Players can collect telemetry and change vehicle parameters, yet still struggle to explain what happened and which change should come next. ApexTuner brings these steps together in one repeatable workflow.

01 / PLAN

Build a setup with context

Select a car, circuit and event constraints. Generate an initial, explainable setup proposal while preserving known in-game limits and unknowns.

02 / MEASURE

Capture real driving evidence

Record locally available game telemetry, organize laps and compare driving sessions with associated setup versions.

03 / LEARN

Evaluate what changed

Review lap and handling evidence, identify plausible mechanical issues, and guide the next controlled setup experiment.

Transparent development status

Current prototype

What exists

  • Local GT7 telemetry capture and lap/session processing.
  • Car and circuit recognition with configurable references.
  • Versioned setup records, PP-oriented planning, and rule-based diagnostics.
  • Web interface for reviewing setups and lap comparisons.
  • English and Turkish UI support.
Planned Claude integration

What we aim to build

  • Natural-language engineering briefings grounded in measured sessions.
  • Conversational follow-up questions about driving behavior and setup trade-offs.
  • Structured, evidence-linked change proposals with explicit uncertainty.
  • Feedback loops that compare a suggested adjustment against the next run.
  • Optional spoken coaching after a session, subject to validation.

Not a lap-time simulator and not a guarantee of faster laps. Recommendations remain hypotheses until checked in the game. AI-powered coaching is a roadmap feature, not yet a shipped capability.
